Privacy Policy for Serevo

Effective Date: October 26, 2026 | Last Updated: October 26, 2026
Privacy-First Commitment: Serevo is designed with end-to-end privacy. Your personal journal entries and mood check-ins are encrypted locally on your device using AES-256 and are never sold or shared with advertisers.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

A. Local Device Data & Offline Storage (AES-256 Encrypted)

Mood Check-ins & Daily Journal Entries: All thoughts, reflections, and wellness check-ins are saved locally on your phone in an encrypted database. We cannot read or decrypt your personal journal entries.

Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT): Device biometrics are handled exclusively by your Android device's secure hardware keystore. Serevo never stores or transmits biometric credentials.

B. Device Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used only when you choose to record an audio note or vocal check-in. Audio recordings are encrypted locally and never recorded in the background.
  • Push Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to send optional daily wellness check-ins, routine reminders, and verified crisis alerts.

C. Account Information

If you register for community mutual aid or counselor communication, we collect your display name and contact credentials (email or phone). All network traffic uses modern Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).

D. Crash Reporting & Diagnostics

We use Sentry to capture anonymized diagnostic stack traces to identify crashes and stability issues. Diagnostic reports are scrubbed of any personal journaling or sensitive text.

2. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your information. We do not integrate third-party advertising tracking SDKs.

3. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You can clear all local data directly within the app settings. You can request permanent deletion of any cloud-associated account data by visiting Settings > Privacy > Delete Account in the app or emailing privacy@serevo.org.zw.
