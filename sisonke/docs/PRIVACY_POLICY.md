# Privacy Policy for Serevo

**Last Updated:** October 26, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 26, 2026  

Welcome to **Serevo** ("we," "our," or "us"). Serevo is a mental health, youth peer-support, and community mutual aid application developed to provide safe, accessible, and privacy-respecting wellness tools. 

Your privacy is our core foundational value. This Privacy Policy details how Serevo handles information, how your data is protected on your device, and your rights regarding your personal information.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

### A. Local Device Data & Offline Storage (AES-256 Encrypted)
- **Mood Check-ins & Daily Journal Entries:** All thoughts, emotions, and journal entries you enter are stored locally on your device in an encrypted Isar database using AES-256 encryption. We cannot read or access your private journal entries.
- **Biometric Authentication:** If you enable Biometric / Fingerprint login (`USE_BIOMETRIC`, `USE_FINGERPRINT`), this authentication is handled exclusively by your Android operating system's secure hardware enclave. Serevo never accesses, stores, or transmits your biometric samples.

### B. Device Permissions
- **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** Used only when you choose to record a voice note or participate in guided audio check-ins. Voice notes are stored locally and encrypted. Audio is never recorded in the background or shared with third-party advertisers.
- **Push Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used solely to send optional daily check-in prompts, wellness reminders, and urgent community support alerts. You can disable notifications anytime in your Android device settings.

### C. Account Information (Optional Sync)
If you register for community features, peer groups, or mutual aid pools:
- We may collect your display name, email, or telephone number to authenticate your identity.
- All communications with backend servers use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).

### D. Crash Reporting & Diagnostics
- We use **Sentry** to capture anonymized crash telemetry (e.g. device model, OS version, stack trace) to fix bugs and improve performance.
- Sentry telemetry is strictly scrubbed of all journal content, passwords, and private communications.

---

## 2. Data Sharing and Third Parties
- **We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your data.**
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** Serevo does not include third-party advertising SDKs or tracking pixels.
- **Law Enforcement & Emergencies:** In life-threatening emergencies involving immediate self-harm or violence where emergency contacts or crisis dispatch are summoned by you, emergency information may be routed to designated verified crisis helplines.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Account Deletion

- **Local Storage:** You can wipe all local logs, entries, and caches at any time directly through the app settings or by clearing the app data in Android Settings.
- **Account & Cloud Deletion:** You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and associated cloud records. You can initiate this in the app under **Settings > Privacy > Delete Account** or by contacting our Data Protection Officer at `privacy@serevo.org.zw`. Requests are processed within 14 business days.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy
Serevo is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will promptly delete it.

---

## 5. Security Safeguards
We implement multi-layered security controls:
- End-to-end TLS/WSS encryption in transit.
- AES-256 local database encryption.
- Android system FlagSecure screen capture protection on sensitive screens.
